New Recruitment System

The recruitment system has been completely reworked. Be sure to review the changes carefully.

Recruitment Tiers

Recruitment times are now tiered into three categories:

  • Levy and basic units - 2 turns
  • Professional units - 3 turns
  • Elite units - 4 turns

Military Infrastructure

Barracks, ranges, and stables have been replaced by the Military Industry Complex (MIC) chain and garrison chain. Each MIC level determines troop quality:

  • Training Grounds - Recruit levy and basic units
  • Basic Armoury - Unlock professional units
  • Barracks and Armoury - Unlock elite units (+1 recruitment point)
  • Barracks and Foundry Complex - Same as Barracks and Armoury, but with +2 recruitment points

Each recruitment point reduces by one turns required to train a unit. Garrison chain only enables recruiting of most basic units.

In regions with homeland government types, elite units are available at Basic Armoury. Similarly, all Area of Recruitment (AOR) units, including elites, are unlocked at Basic Armoury, ensuring every faction starts with access to its best troops.

Government & Colonies

Conquering a region alone won’t allow troop recruitment. You'll need basic control of the land and manpower by establishing a government. If the region’s population isn’t of your ethnicity, building a colony is required to unlock recruitment for faction-specific troops.

Example: As a Greek faction like Boeotia, conquering an Aetolian region lets you recruit generic Greek troops, but Boeotian-specific units require a colony. In Celtic regions, colonies are needed for all Greek units since local manpower is unavailable.

Note: This mechanic applies only to the player. AI factions won’t construct governments or colonies due to technical limitations.

Government Types

Governments determine troop recruitment options based on integration levels. There are four types:

Dependency - Full AOR roster; no factional units.

Indirect Rule - Basic and professional factional and AOR troops. Non-local factional units require Colony Level 1.

Direct Rule - All factional units and basic AOR troops. Elite factional units require Colony Level 2 for non-local ethnicities.

Homeland - Full access to all factional units and basic AOR troops without colonies. Early access to elite troops in homeland regions.

Governments also impact other aspects of gameplay. Check building descriptions for details.

Tip: To see locally available AOR units and requirements, check the Region Information Building in each settlement. This will also help you explore factional troops for each government type before construction.

Heavy Cavalry and Camel factional units

Only for your factional units outsides homeland, you will need to provide fine horses and camels in order to allow its recruitment. That is fulfilled if the region has the resources present or if you build the supply level of its resource exploitation building in any of your regions.

Siege and Blockade Effects

When a settlement is under siege for 1 turn, the following penalties are applied:

  • 5% tax penalty
  • Trade suspended
  • 2.5% penalty to population growth

Since 2 turns of being under siege and until the sixth turn, the penalties increase considerably:

  • Additional 5% tax penalty
  • Additional 2.5% penalty to population growth

Blockade Effects

  • Trade suspended
  • 2.5% penalty to population growth

Optimizing Campaign AI Performance

To ensure optimal AI behavior and maintain a dynamic campaign experience, it is advisable to save and reload your game frequently. This action addresses a known issue where the AI may become passive.

For best results, reload your saved game every 8 turns.

New Growth and Economy System

The growth and resource systems have been revamped to add more depth and strategy. Growing your settlements will now be more challenging, making it take longer to upgrade them and creating a clearer distinction between large cities and small towns. There are now 10 different farming building chains, but usually regions will only have 1 or 2 of them available due the local terrain, climate and your factions agricultural specializations. In most cases, you get to choose between a crop variant (better growth) and an animal husbandry variant (better income). All farms can also be further boosted by the resources present, meaning there is no best option in every case. The terrain not only determines what options you have available in a region but also how far it can be upgraded. It is therefore advised to look at the building browser before choosing.

Sanitation has been split into a water&sanitation and a healing chain. For 'civilized' factions the water&sanitation line has greater growth potential, but it's more expensive and regions without a source of fresh water will not be able to fully upgrade it.

You can force boost growth by importing grain into your settlements, but you must conquer and develop grain exporting regions for that first.

The resource system has also been overhauled! There are now 41 resources representing major trade goods of the time, some of which can enhance specific buildings with bonuses, like grain for farms or metals for smiths and workshops. Additionally, you can choose to exploit resources in a region through unique building chains for each one. Each resource chain is unique: some have two or three tiers, and others even have a special supply level available with a set of requirements, providing bonuses to your capital or your entire faction.

This means you'll need to plan which settlements to grow or capture to get these strategic buildings!

Tip: put any building in the building queue (and only that one) and look at the 'Settlement income' and 'Settlement details' tabs to see its complete effects highlighted in green and red.

Special Resource Exploitation Levels

Some resource exploitation building chains include a special export level at the end of their chain that offer unique strategic bonuses, and you can only build one of each type across your realm. Effects on other buildings can will also be fulfilled by the resource being available locally, in which case the bonusses will not stack if you also have a export level built in your faction. Controlling and developing key settlements is essential to unlocking these powerful features:

Lead: +5% health bonus to public baths and aquaducts factionwide and +10% trade for Metalworkers factionwide

Wine: +5% happiness factionwide.

Tin: +10% trade bonus factionwide, +20 trade to Metalworkers trade income factionwide if combined with copper.

Copper: +10% trade bonus factionwide, +20 trade to Metalworkers trade income factionwide if combined with tin.

Iron: Enables weapon and armor upgrades and reduces Weaponsmiths tax penalty factionwide.

Purple Dye: +5% law bonus factionwide to all cities (minor, large, huge) and 10% trade bonus for tier 2, 3 textiles building.

Marble: 10% construction cost discount for temples factionwide and 10% construction cost discount for civic buildings in your capital.

Horses: Enables factional heavy cavalry recruitment factionwide and increased happiness from horse racing buildings factionwide.

Camels: Enables factional camel units production factionwide.

Papyrus: +5% law bonus factionwide.

Salt: +10% trade for tier 2, 3, 4, 5 animal husbandry farms and fish salting buildings factionwide and +3% tax bonus factionwide.

Silk: +10% trade bonus factionwide.

Incense: +10% happiness factionwide.

Amber: +10% culture conversion bonus factionwide.

Medicinal herbs & Perfumes: +5% health to tier 2 and 3 healers factionwide.

Wild Animals: +5% happiness bonus factionwide.

Pitch, Bitumen & Tars: +1 attack for slingers and ships for ports factionwide (can stack with timber and hemp).

Hemp: +1 attack for slingers and ships for ports factionwide (can stack with timber and pitch).

Timber: +1 attack for slingers and ships for ports factionwide (can stack with pitch and hemp).

Slave Trade: tax bonus to high tier farms, mines, quarries, salt exploitation, Sulphur: +1 farming for tier 3, 4, 5 cropfarms.

Stone: +1 construction point in your capital (reduces building time by one turn) but also -10 tax in capital.

Honey: +2 health (10% happiness and 1% growth) in your capital.

Jewelry: +10% trade bonus in your capital.

Olive Oil: +1% population growth bonus in your capital.

Spices: +10% trade bonus in your capital.
