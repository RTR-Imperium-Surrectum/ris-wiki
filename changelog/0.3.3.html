What's Changed (v. 0.3.3)

  • Fixed night luts for battles and campaign map as they were too dark
  • Fixed crash when playing on languages other than English
  • Moved resources in Italy to allow easier placement of watchtowers
  • Fixed flickering skies
  • Darkened textures that were too bright
  • Added PBR textures to agents
  • Lots of tweaks to luts and lighting
  • Tweaked economy to reduce snowballing
  • Changed dummies faction's culture to stop them gaining Roman senate traits
  • Fixed bug with incorrect Elephant unit sizes
  • Fixed bug with Galatian agent names
  • Small update to starting diplomatic situation
  • Reduced bonuses for Spartan kings
  • Reduced chances of accumulating Lewd trait
  • Fixed incorrect effect descriptions on some ancillaries
  • Fixed the pink trees (for real)!
