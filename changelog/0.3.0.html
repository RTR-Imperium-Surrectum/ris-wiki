RIS 0.3.0

What's Changed (v. 0.3.0)

  • 4 New Factions (Galatians, Sparta, Pergamon, Bosporan Kingdom, 22 total now including the Free Peoples).
  • Added unit texture variation to nearly all Pre-Marian Roman, Carthaginian, Arevacian and Macedonian units, including over 130 new hi-resolution shield variants.
  • New BI-based religion system, simulating Cultures (Greco-Roman, Eastern, Tribal).
  • More traits from VE mod including Ethnic, Dynastic and City State Traits as well as a Spartan Agoge system, and Overstretch System for armies in enemy lands
  • Condensed factions and changed to more historical faction names:
    • Parni instead of Parthia
    • Siraces instead of Sarmatians
    • Arevaci instead of Celtiberians
    • Arverni instead of Gauls
    • Massylii instead of Numidia
  • Arverni are now a one settlement faction, centred on Gergovia.
  • Parni start as a horde, Siraces can horde.
  • 28 new regions along with updated victory conditions for each faction.
  • More historical family trees for most factions, and multiple family trees for some.
  • Shield wall ability from BI implemented for Hoplite units.
  • The ai_do_not_attack_faction feature from ALX was used on Massylii/Carthage & Parni/Siraces.
  • Edited dead space of map so Ptolemies/Carthage don't wage war and nobody goes north of the Caspian Sea.
  • Overhauled diplomacy and faction relations.
  • New descriptions for many units and factions.
  • New loading screens that don't consistently crash the game!
  • BI events and sounds for hording and religion.
  • Music fixed and adjusted to play more variety.
  • Campaign map music was added for Carthage and Massylii.
  • Wives & children portraits added from OG RTW for flavour.
  • New battle map bushes and rocks.
  • Added tree lods for performance.
  • All new realistic battle map ground textures.
  • Many battle settlement textures changed.
  • Edited particles, sun flare, and those little things that make the game look great!
  • Added pbrs for all units for better unit quality and performance optimization.
  • Many image optimizations and compressions as well as bug fixes to make mod as stable as possible.
  • Many campaign balance optimisations.
