What's Changed (v. 0.3.2)

  • Bosporans can recruit Biremes
  • Trade fleets now available
  • Bosporan unit costs fixed
  • Scythian Royal Infantry don’t charge after shooting arrows
  • Seleucid Elephants upkeep lowered
  • Seleucid capital moved to Seleucia
  • Distance to capital penalty lowered
  • Rebalance of culture in eastern Seleucid regions to allow saving from revolt
  • Added Bactrian family tree to the seleucids
  • Upgraded many Seleucid gov buildings to annexation to earn more money
  • Fixed volcaean axemen ctd
  • Overhauled diplomacy based on historical records
  • Parni now settle in historical homelands instead of Mesopotamia
  • Rebalanced tax income and bonuses to prevent ai from blowing up and having death stacks everywhere
  • Removed all allied and occupation buildings from non Roman factions, building tree clearer now
  • Split Roman recruitment into two trees, cannot build both
  • Fixed enhanced_tweaks to not be so bright and painful
  • Fixed temples
  • Fixed flickering units
  • Fixed invisible stone walls on the strat map
  • Tweaked recruitment priorities for certain units
  • Fixed pink trees
