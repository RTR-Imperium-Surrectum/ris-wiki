What's Changed (v. 0.2.1)

  • Pink Units have been fixed and the textures should all show now.
  • Missing Unit Cards have been found and added in.
  • Invisible Units have been fixed and can now be seen.
  • Improved Vegetation. so the crazy polygonal UFO trees are fixed, and overall stability has improved.
  • Redid Rebel Symbol, it's no longer too similar to the Seleucid one.
  • Added more rebel banners for battles, replacing the vanilla ones.
  • Fixed Marian reforms trait triggers.
  • Added more music and packed it, this should help overall stability.
  • Fixed cavalry hiding in long grass.
  • Fixed light infantry and missile units not hiding in long grass.
  • Corrected horses on Syracusan cavalry.
  • Reduced darkness on night time campaign map.
  • Improved rain and snow visibility.
  • Reduced scripted garrison strength.
  • Fixed Insubres infantry shoulder armour.
  • Fixed Celtic swordsman shield angle.
  • Fixed mercenary Celtic infantry description.
  • Modded descr_geography.db to increase ground terrain realism and diversity.
  • Fixed Thureophoroi and Thorakitai spear and shield angles.
