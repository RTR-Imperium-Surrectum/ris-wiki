Areas of recruitment (AOR)

← wiki index · game guides · recruitment zones

An area of recruitment is a group of regions with troops of their own. Any faction that holds a region in the area can raise them there, alongside its own faction units. RIS has 172 areas and 439 AOR units; every area, its units and its map are on recruitment zones.

Early access to elite troops in homeland regions.

In regions with homeland government types, elite units are available at Basic Armoury. Similarly, all Area of Recruitment (AOR) units, including elites, are unlocked at Basic Armoury, ensuring every faction starts with access to its best troops.

Where areas overlap, the broader one steps aside: a unit of a general area is barred from regions that also belong to a more specific one, so the local speciality is raised there instead.
