Retinue

← wiki index · all traits · all factions

A character in RTR: Imperium Surrectum gathers a retinue: 1,118 followers, keepsakes, offices and animals that attach themselves to generals, governors and agents and travel with them. Where a trait is who a character has become, retinue is who and what he keeps around him, and members can be traded between characters who meet.

How retinue works

  • Most members are earned in play. They join a character when he does the right thing in

the right place: wins a battle, governs a city with a school, holds an office. A few are granted only by events.

  • Traits attract retinue. What a character is shapes who seeks him out: the drunkard

attracts drinking companions, the scholar attracts philosophers. Trait and retinue pages link both ways.

  • Some members are unique: only one exists in the world at a time.
  • Some members exclude others: rival followers who will not share a tent.
