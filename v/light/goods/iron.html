  • Lets you build: 9 building levels: Blacksmith (Industry), Armourer (Industry), Foundry (Industry), Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry), Metalwork Artisans (Industry), Metalworking District (Industry), Mines (Industry), Deep Mining Complex (Industry), Iron Exports (Special exports)
