In game: Allows the trading of fish

What it is

KindTraded goodTier1Trade value1

What it does

  • Lets you build: 3 building levels: Fish Salter (Urban), Salted Fish Market (Urban), Garum Factory (Urban)
  • Bonuses where it is present: farming level +2 (Reedland Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +4 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +6 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry)); population growth +1 (Trade Port); population growth +1 (Shipwright); population growth +1 (Dockyard)
  • Bonuses only where it is absent: farming level +1 (Reedland Pasturing (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +3 (Seasonal Pasture Rotation (Adaptive husbandry)); farming level +5 (Managed Wetland Pastures (Adaptive husbandry))
