Slaves

Main campaignFour RomansRIS Light

← all trade goods · all regions · wiki index

In game: Slaves Distributed across all settlements with governors. Resource is exhausted after 10 turns

What it is

KindSlaves: taken by enslaving the people of a conquered settlement.Trade value1Groupslive goods, slavesDepletableyes, exhausted after 10 turns, 1,000 population per unit

What it does

Slaves come from enslaving the people of a settlement you capture. They are shared out across all your settlements that have a governor, and each lot is used up after 10 turns; one lot stands for 1,000 people. No building or unit in RIS requires slaves.

Where it is found

418 sites on the map, 418 in total quantity, across a number of regions this generator declines to state: the two ways of deriving it disagree.
