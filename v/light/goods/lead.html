What it is

KindMined: a mine can be built at the settlement that holds it.Tier3Trade value1Groupsbase minerals, all minerals
When minedLead Mine: A valuable source of income

What it does

  • Lets you build: 6 building levels: Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry), Metalwork Artisans (Industry), Metalworking District (Industry), Mines (Industry), Deep Mining Complex (Industry), Lead Exports (Special exports)
  • Bonuses where it is present: taxable income +10 (Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry)); taxable income +16 (Metalwork Artisans (Industry)); taxable income +24 (Metalworking District (Industry)); population health +5 (Public Baths (Sanitation)); population health +7 (Aqueduct (Sanitation)); population health +8 (Great Bathhouse (Sanitation))
  • Bonuses only where it is absent: taxable income +8 (Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry)); taxable income +12 (Metalwork Artisans (Industry)); taxable income +18 (Metalworking District (Industry)); population health +4 (Public Baths (Sanitation)); population health +6 (Aqueduct (Sanitation)); population health +7 (Great Bathhouse (Sanitation))

22 sites on the map, 39 in total quantity, across a number of regions this generator declines to state: the two ways of deriving it disagree.
