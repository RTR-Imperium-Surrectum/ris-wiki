What it does

  • Lets you build: 4 building levels: Murex Snail Collectors (Industry), Purple Dye Works (Industry), Purple Dye Factory (Industry), Purple Dye Exports (Industry)
  • Bonuses where it is present: trade income +3 to +4 (The Cothon of Carthage); trade income +3 (Textile Factory (Urban)); trade income +3 to +4 (Luxury Clothiers Quarter(Urban))
  • Bonuses only where it is absent: trade income +2 to +3 (The Cothon of Carthage); trade income +2 (Textile Factory (Urban)); trade income +2 to +3 (Luxury Clothiers Quarter(Urban))
