What it does

  • Lets you build: 7 building levels: Blacksmith (Industry), Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry), Metalwork Artisans (Industry), Metalworking District (Industry), Mines (Industry), Deep Mining Complex (Industry), Copper Exports (Special exports)
  • Bonuses where it is present: armour upgrade +1 (Blacksmith (Industry)); armour upgrade +1 (Armourer (Industry)); armour upgrade +1 (Foundry (Industry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry)); trade income +2 to +3 (Metalwork Artisans (Industry)); trade income +3 to +4 (Metalworking District (Industry)); taxable income +10 (Local Mint (Civic)); taxable income +14 (State Mint (Civic)); taxable income +20 (Great Mint (Civic))
  • Bonuses only where it is absent: trade income +1 (Toolmakers' Workshop (Industry)); trade income +1 to +2 (Metalwork Artisans (Industry)); trade income +2 to +3 (Metalworking District (Industry)); taxable income +6 (Local Mint (Civic)); taxable income +10 to +14 (State Mint (Civic)); taxable income +14 to +20 (Great Mint (Civic))
