Main campaignRIS LightRIS Classic

← all regions and settlements · wiki index

A region's water source. A river, lake, springs or oasis lets you build irrigated farming and the Large Colony; an aquifer feeds the qanat chain instead. Having any of them rules the rainfed farming chain out.

River

On 357 regions.

  • Lets you build: 14 building levels: Large Colony, Farms, Farms+1, Farms+2, Farms+3, Farms+4, Irrigation Ditches (Crops), Basin Irrigation (Crops), Canal Network (Crops), Irrigation Aqueducts (Crops), Irrigated Great Estates (Crops), Public Baths (Sanitation), Aqueduct (Sanitation), Great Bathhouse (Sanitation)
  • Blocks: 5 building levels: Land Clearance (Crops), Contour Furrows (Crops), Runoff Water Collection (Crops), Crop Rotation (Crops), Great Estates (Crops)
  • Numeric effects where it is present: taxable income +6 (Milling District (Urban)); taxable income +6 to +10 (Brewing Quarter (Urban)); taxable income +10 to +15 (Baking Guild (Urban))
  • Numeric effects it withholds (granted only where it is absent): taxable income +3 (Milling District (Urban)); taxable income +6 (Brewing Quarter (Urban)); taxable income +10 (Baking Guild (Urban))
