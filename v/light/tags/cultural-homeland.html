Cultural homelands

Main campaignFour RomansRIS LightRIS Classic

← all regions and settlements · wiki index

A homeland is the land a faction comes from. Every homeland does the same thing:

  • Only the faction the homeland belongs to benefits, and only it can install the Homeland government there. Anyone else holding the region gets nothing from it.
  • In your own homeland you cannot build Abandon Settlement, Dependency, Indirect Rule, Direct Rule, Small Colony, Large Colony, Local Mint (Civic). It cannot be given a lesser government, made a colony, or given a local mint.
