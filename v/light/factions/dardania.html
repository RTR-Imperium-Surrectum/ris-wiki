The campaign brief

Congrats! You unlocked this faction for playing, which means, you just modded! How about that?

Starting diplomacy

Dardania begins the campaign with no allies, no trade agreements and no wars.

Like every faction, it is also at war with the Free Peoples, who hold every settlement no faction does.
