What it unlocks

Nothing. No building and no unit depends on a belief. Nor does any belief cause unrest against another in RIS, whether they share a group or not.

Its group

The Hellenic group holds 12 beliefs. The others are Aeolian, Arcadian, Bosporan, Cypriot Greek, Epirote, Greco-Bactrian, Indo-Greek, Ionian, Macedonian, Northwest Greek, Pamphylian Greek.
