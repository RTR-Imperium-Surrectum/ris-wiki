Part of the Scythian group. Held in 43 of 547 regions · its people live in 47 · the state belief of 11 factions

Where it is on the map

43 of the 547 regions hold this belief, 7.9% of the map. In some it is the majority belief; in the rest it is a minority held alongside a larger one.

What it unlocks

Nothing. No building and no unit depends on a belief. Nor does any belief cause unrest against another in RIS, whether they share a group or not.

Its group

The Scythian group holds this belief alone.
