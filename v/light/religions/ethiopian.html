Where it is on the map

22 of the 547 regions hold this belief, 4.0% of the map. In some it is the majority belief; in the rest it is a minority held alongside a larger one.

What it unlocks

Nothing. No building and no unit depends on a belief. Nor does any belief cause unrest against another in RIS, whether they share a group or not.

Its group

The Ethiopian group holds this belief alone.
