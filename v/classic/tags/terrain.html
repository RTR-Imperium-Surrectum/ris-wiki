Terrain

Main campaignRIS LightRIS Classic

← all regions and settlements · wiki index

Every region on the map carries one terrain tag. Terrain decides which of the mod's competing land-use chains you may build (farms, irrigated farming, rainfed farming, the four pastoralism chains, qanats, marsh reclamation), and several of them carry a standing penalty.
