Thracian Sica Cavalry Reforms

Main campaignRIS LightRIS Classic

Who gets it: Odrysian Kingdom, Cabyle, Asti, Antigonid Kingdom, Seleucid Empire, Seleucid Dynasts, Seleucid Satraps, Ptolemaic Kingdom, Ptolemaic Dynasts and Pergamon

Hail, great king! Over time, our soldiers have encountered and slain many foes. This has also resulted in adopting new military strategies and equipment, that are represented through a number of reformed units that have now become available for recruitment.

How to get it

It fires when it is turn 78 (251 BC) or later.

The game checks this at the end of every round.

What it unlocks

2 units can be recruited once it fires:
