What it unlocks

Nothing. No building and no unit depends on a belief. Nor does any belief cause unrest against another in RIS, whether they share a group or not.

Its group

The Hellenic group holds 9 beliefs. The others are Aeolian, Arcadian, Bosporan, Epirote, Greco-Bactrian, Ionian, Macedonian, Northwest Greek.
