Where it is on the map

No region on the map holds this belief at the campaign start.

The people it belongs to

No region lists Celtic people among its population.

Who follows it

63 of the 239 factions hold this as their state belief. Between them they hold 17 settlements at the campaign start.

What it unlocks

Nothing. No building and no unit depends on a belief. Nor does any belief cause unrest against another in RIS, whether they share a group or not.

Its group

The Celtic group holds this belief alone.
