160 regions, each with one settlement, every one held by a faction at the campaign start. 55 settlements are a faction's capital, marked ★. The region is the land (terrain, fertility, trade goods); the settlement is the town (its size, manpower and buildings).

Size is one of 5 levels, and it decides what a settlement can build and raise.
