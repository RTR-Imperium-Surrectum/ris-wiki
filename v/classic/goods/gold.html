What it is

KindMined: a mine can be built at the settlement that holds it.Trade value3Groupsprecious minerals, all mineralsWhen minedGold Mine: A valuable source of income

What it does

  • Lets you build: 6 building levels: Jewellery Crafter (Industry), Artisan Jeweller (Industry), Jewellers Quarter (Industry), Jewellery Exports (Industry), Mines (Industry), Deep Mining Complex (Industry)
  • Bonuses where it is present: trade income +1 to +2 (Jewellery Crafter (Industry)); trade income +3 to +4 (Artisan Jeweller (Industry)); trade income +4 to +5 (Jewellers Quarter (Industry)); trade income +5 to +7 (Jewellery Exports (Industry)); mine income -15 to +15 (Mines (Industry)); mine income -25 to +25 (Deep Mining Complex (Industry)); trade income +3 (Local Mint (Civic)); trade income +2 (State Mint (Civic)); public order (law) +2 (Great Mint (Civic)); trade income +3 (Great Mint (Civic))
  • Bonuses only where it is absent: trade income +1 (Jewellery Crafter (Industry)); trade income +3 (Artisan Jeweller (Industry)); trade income +4 (Jewellers Quarter (Industry)); trade income +5 (Jewellery Exports (Industry)); mine income -12 to +12 (Mines (Industry)); mine income -20 to +20 (Deep Mining Complex (Industry)); trade income +2 to +3 (Local Mint (Civic)); trade income +1 to +2 (State Mint (Civic)); public order (law) +1 (Great Mint (Civic)); trade income +2 to +3 (Great Mint (Civic))
