All factionsRemastered

A remastered faction's units are the mod's own new models, with seven soldier models per unit, instead of older models with new textures. 81 of the 230 playable factions are remastered. Everywhere on this wiki they carry a red R after their name.

Counted from the units each faction can raise as its own, leaving out the peasant levy and the ships, which every faction shares. A faction counts as remastered when most of its own units are.
