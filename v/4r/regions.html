Regions and settlements

← wiki index · sortable list · world map

1,305 regions, each with one settlement, every one held by a faction at the campaign start. 215 settlements are a faction's capital. The region is the land (terrain, fertility, trade goods); the settlement is the town (its size, manpower and buildings).

Settlements

Size is one of 5 levels, and it decides what a settlement can build and raise.

The land

  • Terrain: one per region; decides which land-use chains can be built
  • Climate: one per region, alongside terrain; mostly acts through the rainfed farming level
  • Water sources: river, lake, springs, oasis or aquifer; gates irrigated farming, the Large Colony and qanats
  • Fertility: how rich the farmland is
  • Ports: the natural harbour, which caps the port chain
  • River trade: river trade and hazards
  • Trade goods: the resources a region produces
  • Wonders: the Seven Wonders and where they stand
