Buildings

← wiki index

The economic and growth systems of RIS are more in-depth than in the base game.

Growing your settlements is now more challenging, making it take longer to upgrade them and creating a greater distinction between large cities and small towns. Many buildings are mutually exclusive: only one from each category can be built per settlement, so you will need to choose what's best for every region. Many buildings are already present at game start, but some of those might not be what you want or what is most efficient, so you may want to replace them. Every region has a building called 'Region Information Scroll'. This building shows useful information about the region, but it also houses many modifiers of mod mechanics that you should ignore.

Important Tip: put a building in the building queue (only one, to avoid confusion) and the real economic, growth and public order effects appear in the 'Settlement income' and 'Settlement details' tabs, highlighted in green and red.

81 building chains, 266 levels in all.

Some buildings rule others out: once one is built, the other cannot be built in that settlement. The *Excl.* column counts the levels in a chain that do this, and each page marks them.

Farming

There are 10 different farming building chains, 5 crop options and 5 animal husbandry options, but regions will only ever have 2 of them available to choose from, 1 crop and 1 husbandry farm (though some regions with a climate hostile to crops will only have the husbandry option). Which ones are available out of the 10 variants is determined by the local terrain, climate, water availability and your faction's agricultural knowledge. You can check the terrain, climate and water availability in the 'Region Information Scroll' building. Note that fertility does not give any growth in RIS; a region's natural suitability for growth is modelled through the farming and sanitation buildings. Generally, the crop variants offer better growth and the animal husbandry variants offer better income. The terrain not only determines what options you have available in a region but also how far it can be upgraded, so it is worth looking at the building browser before choosing. All farms can also be further boosted by the resources present, which is shown in text under the building effects, meaning what is best in one region may not necessarily be best everywhere. All additional bonuses from resources and such are already included in the effects listed, so a +5 farming figure can be made up of +4 base and +1 from a resource.

Food trade

You can force-boost growth in your settlements by importing grain, which is done by producing and consuming grain surplus points. All factions start with 1 point for free, and granaries (grain export buildings) produce additional points; these buildings are available in regions with the grain resource and with crop farms built. The maximum number of points you can reach is 16. You can check your current number of grain points in the Region Information Scroll of your capital. Grain imports consume these factionwide points to provide growth and happiness where they are built. Higher levels of both the grain import and grain export chains also require sea or river ports to move all that bulky grain.

Ports

How far ports can be upgraded is limited by region, based on natural harbour availability. You can see what the limit is for a coastal region in the Region Information Scroll. However, by building the 'Harbour Improvement' you can exceed that natural limit by 1 level. This building self-destructs once completed, and your limit is raised from that point on. Note that ships also require either Training Grounds or Garrison 1. Ships can be upgraded by ports that have access to hemp, timber or pitch (all of these can be supplied factionwide to all your ports).

River Ports

Only available where there is a navigable river, and mutually exclusive with regular ports; this is shown in the Region Information Scroll building. Note that this river resource is not the same as the one used for farms and sanitation, as not every river that could be used for irrigation and sanitation was navigable.

Health

Sanitation has been split into a water & sanitation chain and a healing chain. Many factions do not get access to the final upgrades. For 'civilized' factions the water & sanitation line has greater growth potential, but it is more expensive, and regions without a source of fresh water will not be able to fully upgrade it. The healing chain can be buffed by more resources, though, and can be a more cost-effective option when those resources are present. It also offers valuable healing ancillaries.

Civics

Civic buildings are powerful buildings that only unlock at city level. Many provide law, a powerful stat as it combats corruption, and academies greatly improve your characters. Note that 'civilian buildings' construction bonuses are not limited to these civic buildings: they affect all buildings that are not walls, military (mic/garrison) or temples.

Temples

The current temple system is a placeholder. As it stands, temples spread your own culture when the government is either direct rule or homeland, and on the highest tier they even spread your culture factionwide. A temple instead spreads the region's local culture when the 'builder' faction is not your own, in other words if it was pre-existing and its building culture does not match yours. Tip: hover over the building to see the building culture. Lastly, temples give additional happiness if your culture is the dominant culture in the region.
